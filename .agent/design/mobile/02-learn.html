cohesive

adjectiveneutral
Saved

Forming a united whole; when the parts fit and work together well.

বাংলাসুসংহত, সংঘবদ্ধ

How it's used

Used for teams, groups, writing and designs whose parts connect well. Common in academic writing (“a cohesive argument”) and in software (“a cohesive module” does one clear job).

Collocations
cohesive teamcohesive unitcohesive argumenthighly cohesive
Patterns
  • be cohesive
  • form a cohesive whole
  • make sth more cohesive
Examples
  1. After the retreat, the team felt much more cohesive.
  2. Your essay needs a more cohesive structure.
  3. A highly cohesive module does one job well.
  4. The new branding gives the site a cohesive look.
Don't confuse

coherent — clear and logical, easy to follow.
