cohesive

adjectiveneutral

Forming a united whole; when the parts fit and work together well.

বাংলাসুসংহত, সংঘবদ্ধ

How it's used

Used for teams, groups, writing and designs whose parts connect well. Common in academic writing and in software (“a cohesive module”).

Collocations
cohesive teamcohesive unitcohesive argumenthighly cohesive
Examples
  1. After the retreat, the team felt much more cohesive.
  2. Your essay needs a more cohesive structure.
