cohesive

adjective neutral
Saved

Forming a united whole; when the parts fit and work together well.

বাংলাসুসংহত, সংঘবদ্ধ

How it's used

Used for teams, groups, writing and designs whose parts connect well. Very common in academic writing (“a cohesive argument”) and in software (“a cohesive module” does one clear job).

Collocations
cohesive teamcohesive unitcohesive argumentcohesive designhighly cohesive
Patterns
  • be cohesive
  • form a cohesive whole
  • make sth more cohesive
Examples
  1. After the retreat, the team felt much more cohesive.
  2. Your essay needs a more cohesive structure; each paragraph should lead to the next.
  3. A highly cohesive module does one job and does it well.
  4. The new branding gives the whole website a cohesive look.
Don't confuse

coherent — clear and logical, easy to follow: “a coherent explanation”.
